What it can run

Eight tasks today. Each one knows how to travel to itself, so a list can cross the map without you — and the ones that are not due yet fade out and are passed over, so a weekly task can sit in a daily list all week.

World 3

Refinery

Ranks up every salt that is ready — and only the ones that are ready — then tells you which material ran out.

World 3

Equinox

Spends a full Equinox bar on the dream you pick. It works out when the bar will next be full from your own save, and sits the rest out rather than spending a teleport to look.

World 7

Sushi Station

Sorts the grid, merges what can be merged, refills with the cook button, and repeats until nothing is left to do.

World 1

Swishy Hoops

Times each shot against the moving hoop, tracks the score, and stops when the lives run out.

World 1

Throwy Darts

Reads the wind and the moving platform on every throw, aiming for bullseyes and the nine-in-a-row streak.

World 6

Summoning

Buys the familiar out to 25/25, holding the button and watching the level rather than counting clicks. Then sleeps until the weekly reset.

World 4

Ribbons

Merges every duplicate on the cooking shelf, one at a time, re-reading after each — because a merge can make a pair that was not there before.

Anywhere

Fighting

Travels to any map in the game and fights there for as long as you set. The map list is read from your save, so it is every map you can reach.

Before you download

This is the part most download pages leave out. Read it first.

Automation may break the game's rules

Legends of Idleon is made by Lava, who sets the terms for how it may be played. This program is not made by, endorsed by, or connected to Lava in any way. Using it could get your account suspended or banned. That risk is yours to take, and it is a real one.

Windows will warn you, and here is how to get past it

The download is not code-signed — a certificate costs more per year than this project has ever made — so SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC” and calls the publisher unknown. That warning is about the missing signature. It does not mean anything was found in the file.

To run it anyway: click More info on that dialog, then the Run anyway button that appears underneath. You will see this once per version.

You do not have to take my word for any of it. Compare what you downloaded against the SHA-256 above — in PowerShell:

Get-FileHash .\IdleonAutomator.exe -Algorithm SHA256

If the hash matches, the file is bit-for-bit what was published here. If it doesn't, don't run it — and please tell me on Discord.

Watch it the first few times

It reads the screen and clicks what it believes it sees, and a misread can cost resources. Press F6 to stop it, twice to force it and get your mouse back. Don't leave it running unattended until you have watched it do the thing you want, correctly, more than once.

What it does with your data

Nothing leaves your computer. There is no account, no telemetry, and no server collecting anything. Your task lists and run history are saved in %APPDATA%\IdleonAutomator, which is also why replacing the program with a newer one keeps them.

The one exception: on startup it asks a small file on Cloudflare whether a newer version exists. That tells Cloudflare your IP address, the way visiting any website does. If there's an update you get a link — it never downloads or installs anything by itself.
